# Public alpha 0.1.0

The first public Lantern Reach tabletop playtest: four books (199 pages total), six printable maps, and public session-feedback and rules-report forms.

Begin with the Player’s Book and Guide’s Book, then run The Silent Berth. The two expansion books are optional.

This is an AI-assisted alpha seeking human table feedback. Automated checks and scripted simulations do not establish actual player enjoyment or balance. Development archives and private source material are excluded from this release.
